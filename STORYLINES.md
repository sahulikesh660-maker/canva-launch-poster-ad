# Three storylines for the Canva 9:16 promo (90 s)

Picked: **1. Launch Poster**. The other two are kept so the film can be re-cut later.

## 1. Launch Poster — PICKED

**Device:** a moment in someone's day; one object (a poster) across apps. **Form:** object-to-object chain with match cuts on cursor clicks.
A small-business owner gets "launch is tomorrow" at 9:41 PM. On a warm, dark desk a blank poster stays blank while downloads pile up. They open Canva, pick a real template, swap the photo, retype the headline, and share a finished poster to a phone. The viewer feels the relief of one place doing the whole job.
**First two seconds:** frame 0 is a phone filling 60% of the frame, screen lit, message "launch is tomorrow" already on screen; the phone lifts 40 px and the message pops — no logo, no black.
**Beats:** hook (phone + message) → tension (files pile up, blank poster, frozen spinner) → reveal (real Canva homepage, title "One idea. One place.") → proof (pick, swap, retype, share) → payoff (finished poster fills frame) → claim ("From idea to shared, in one place.") → end card (Canva mark carried in, "Try Canva free").
**Look:** warm charcoal desk #14121A with a 12% amber light panel → Canva's real white #FFFFFF product plates; Outfit ExtraBold display; one Canva purple #7D2AE8 accent; sharp edges, no 3D tilt of UI.
**Sound:** near-silent tension with muted clicks; confident 118 BPM bed opens on the reveal; key ticks on edits, a pop on share, one riser into the payoff, final chord under the end card. No voiceover.

## 2. One Frame Builds Itself

**Device:** the result first; the object is the explanation. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds, never cuts.
A single vertical canvas is the whole film. Inside it a blank layout fills with a template grid, one template is chosen, elements are dragged in, the headline is typed, and the finished poster resolves; then the frame pulls back to reveal it sitting inside Canva.
**First two seconds:** a blank white canvas at 90% of frame height with a purple outline drawing itself, then a template grid slides up into it.
**Beats:** blank canvas → grid → choose → drag → type → poster → pull-back → claim → end card.
**Look:** white studio void, Canva purple hairlines, type is the whole architecture, physical cards with 0.55 contact shadows.
**Sound:** minimal pulse, soft key ticks, no whooshes; one bright chord on the pull-back.

## 3. Before vs Canva (the bold one)

**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.
The old way churns on the left: tabs, loose font files, a frozen progress bar, a blank poster. Canva stays calm on the right. A cursor drags the finished design across the seam; the old side collapses into one clean card and the frame becomes the finished poster.
**First two seconds:** a hard vertical split — left side crowded and flickering, right side a single clean Canva canvas; a cursor enters from the centre.
**Beats:** split → old way fails → one calm action on the right → design crosses the seam → old side collapses → payoff → claim → end card.
**Look:** cluttered desktop vs Canva white; vermilion #D61F26 only on the old side's failure marks, Canva purple on the result.
**Sound:** frantic muted clicks on the left, steady beat on the right, one drop when the seam collapses.
